## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** Individuals and families in Porur, Chennai and neighboring suburbs seeking reliable, pain-free dental care or routine maintenance.
- **Situation:** Often suffering from dental pain, aesthetic insecurities, or dental anxiety/phobia from past traumatic dental visits; frequently constrained by corporate or family schedules.
- **Job to be Done:** Find a compassionate, qualified dentist nearby, understand treatment options without intimidating medical jargon and quickly book an appointment online or via WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
The website serves as the primary digital home and front door for Dr. Mahe's Dentistry. It informs and reassures prospective patients, demystifies complex dental procedures and provides a frictionless bridge to schedule clinical visits or ask questions. Success is measured by patient trust, reduced pre-appointment anxiety and reliable appointment booking conversions.

## Positioning
Gentle, unhurried, zero-judgment dentistry led by Dr. Maheswari, BDS. Unlike impersonal, high-volume dental clinics, Dr. Mahe's Dentistry emphasizes emotional comfort, patient-paced control (hand-gesture pacing), transparent communication and extended evening hours (open until 10:00 PM) for busy families and professionals.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Core Capabilities:** Interactive symptom / concern explorer, treatment pages (Dental Implants, Root Canal, Orthodontics & Clear Aligners, Pediatric Dentistry, Crowns & Veneers, Oral Surgery, Cosmetic Dentistry), doctor profile & philosophy, clinic location details with interactive map and verified patient reviews.
- **Technical Constraints:** Built on Next.js App Router; static/client-side booking form submission; no direct integration with third-party hospital management EMR/EHR at this stage.
- **Terminology:** "Gentle touch", "Single sitting", "Fear-free child care", "Patient-paced", "Transparent guidance".

## Product Principles
1. **Empathetic Reassurance First:** Prioritize calming language and clear explanations over cold clinical jargon to overcome dental phobia.
2. **Zero Judgment, Total Kindness:** Welcome every patient with warmth and dignity regardless of oral health history or lapse in dental care.
3. **Patient Agency & Control:** Reinforce transparency at every step — patients know what to expect and control the pace of their visit.
4. **Frictionless Action:** Keep booking and direct consultation (WhatsApp/Phone) prominent, immediate and accessible on any device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Form inputs with explicit labels, focus indicators and validation feedback.
- Content written in clear English tailored to local multilingual patient understanding in Chennai.
